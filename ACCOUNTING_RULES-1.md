# Accounting Rules

## Shop Work
Net Profit = Revenue - Purchase Cost
Ahmed = 50% of Net Profit
Abdo = 50% of Net Profit

## Ahmed Work
Net Profit = Revenue - Purchase Cost
Ahmed = 100% of Net Profit
Abdo = 0

## Abdo Work
Net Profit = Revenue - Purchase Cost
Ahmed = 25% of Net Profit
Abdo = 75% of Net Profit
Amount Due From Abdo = Ahmed Share

Purchase cost must never come from customer selling price, `partsCost`, or invoice total.
